# CommuniQ — AI Based Conversation Simulator

CommuniQ is an AI-powered voice-based communication practice app built for Indian students and professionals. It lets you have real, pressure-tested conversations with AI characters so you can sharpen your communication skills before it counts.

---

## What It Does

Most people know they need to communicate better — they just never get a safe space to actually practice. CommuniQ gives you that space.

You speak. An AI character responds. You get evaluated. You improve.

The experience is conversational — you talk naturally, the AI listens, responds, pushes back, and adapts to you in real time. At the end of each session, you receive a detailed performance breakdown.

---

## Who It's For

- **Students** preparing for campus placements, group discussions, and HR rounds
- **Professionals** sharpening their communication before job switches or client-facing roles
- **Anyone** who wants to become a more confident, articulate speaker

---

## Practice Scenarios

**HR Interview**
Practice behavioral questions with an AI HR manager who probes your answers, challenges vague responses, and adapts to your experience level.

**Technical Interview**
Discuss your projects and technical decisions with an AI senior engineer who asks for specifics, trade-offs, and real reasoning — not textbook answers.

**Group Discussion**
Participate in a structured GD with an AI moderator who assigns topics, manages time, and keeps the conversation on track.

**Sales Call**
Pitch to an AI client who is skeptical, distracted, and not easily impressed. Earn their trust turn by turn.

You can also create your own custom scenarios.

---

## How Sessions Work

1. Pick a scenario and difficulty level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced)
2. The AI character speaks first and sets the context
3. You respond naturally by speaking — no typing
4. The AI listens, processes, and responds in a few seconds
5. The conversation continues until you end the session
6. You receive a performance evaluation: scores across clarity, fluency, confidence, vocabulary, and grammar — plus specific strengths and things to work on

---

## Performance Evaluation

After every session you get:

- Scores across 5 dimensions: Clarity, Fluency, Confidence, Vocabulary, Grammar
- A list of filler words you used and how often
- 2 specific strengths from that session
- 2 actionable things to improve
- A recommended next session to continue your progress

---

## Difficulty Levels

- **Beginner** — Patient pacing, simpler follow-ups, supportive flow
- **Intermediate** — Realistic pressure, normal conversational pace
- **Advanced** — Tight follow-ups, zero tolerance for vague answers, high expectations
